## Similarity check, 2026-09-28

Searched: TMview (EUIPO's public search across participating offices,
including UKIPO, EUIPO and WIPO international registrations), plus a web
search. The UKIPO's own search (trademarks.ipo.gov.uk) answered 403 to an
automated browser, and the WIPO Global Brand Database showed a bot check,
so both were covered through TMview only.

**The name "Epiow"**

- **Exact:** one hit in any office. "DHN EPIOW N", CNIPA (China)
  40304156, class 25 (clothing), applicant 广州市亿棉服装有限公司, filed
  2019-08-13, status ended. Different goods, no longer live. No "EPIOW"
  at UKIPO, EUIPO or WIPO.
- **Nearest live mark:** EPIO, WIPO international registration 1691895,
  EPIO Corporation, classes 35 (business management and administration,
  office functions) and 41 (education, training), filed 2022-08-12,
  registered. One letter shorter and close in sound. Epiow would sit in
  classes 9 and 42 (software, SaaS); class 35 overlaps at the edge. This
  is the one mark to watch if Epiow ever files.
- **Other near names (UK, EU or WIPO), none in software:** EPIO
  (UKIPO 36 and WIPO 35/36/41, both expired); EPIOR (WIPO 1901086,
  class 7 engines, 2025); Epion (UK and EU, class 5); PEPIO, GEPIO,
  LEPIO, SAEPIO, DEEPIO, PREPIO, Epione, Epiony, epionce (other goods;
  classes not checked one by one).
- **Web:** no business named Epiow other than ours. Two unrelated spam
  GitHub repositories use the string.

**The mark.** TMview's image search needs an upload through its web
page, which was not run. By eye and by web search, no mark found combines
an open bracket or "C/E" shape with a single dot inside on a squircle
tile; open "C" shapes with a dot are common in logos, so the mark's
distinctiveness rests on the whole: tile, bracket, ember dot and the
wordmark together.

**Conclusion.** No blocking conflict for "Epiow" in software. The
nearest risk is EPIO (WIPO 1691895, classes 35 and 41). Nothing is filed
now, per owner#781.
